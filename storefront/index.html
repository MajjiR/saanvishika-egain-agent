Frequently Asked Questions

What are your pieces made of?

High-quality nickel-free imitation metals with gold or silver plating, CZ/AD stones, kundan and beads. Nothing is real gold or diamond.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes, on orders above ₹499 across India. Orders of ₹499 or less have a flat ₹80 charge.

What is your return policy?

Replacements or refunds for damaged, defective or wrong items, reported within 48 hours (transit damage) or 7 days (defects) with an unboxing video. No change-of-mind returns.

Do you take bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes, from 10 pieces a design with 10-25% off. Ask Mira for an instant quote; 1,000+ pieces are handled by our sales lead.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship in 1-3 business days; delivery takes 3-5 days to metros and 5-7 days elsewhere.

Can you deliver sooner?

Once shipped, our courier sets the date, but Mira can raise a priority ticket with them for you.
