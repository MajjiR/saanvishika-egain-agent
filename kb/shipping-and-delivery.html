Shipping and Delivery

Where do you ship?

We ship across India. If your PIN code is not serviceable, we tell you promptly and give a full refund.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders above ₹499. Orders of ₹499 or less have a flat shipping charge of ₹80. The charge is shown at checkout.

When will my order ship?

Orders ship within 1 to 3 business days after confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays ship on the next working day.

How long does delivery take?

Metro cities: 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Other cities and towns: 5 to 7 business days. Peak seasons such as weddings and festivals, public holidays, or courier problems can cause delays.

How do I track my order?

When your order is dispatched you receive a tracking number by email or SMS. You can also ask our AI assistant for your order status with your order number and the last 4 digits of your phone number.

Can you deliver my order sooner?

Once an order is with our courier partner, we cannot change the delivery date ourselves. The assistant can raise a priority request with the courier and alert our logistics team. Orders not yet shipped can be flagged for priority packing.

What if the address is wrong or nobody is home?

Orders are delivered to the address given at checkout. Please check it carefully. If delivery fails because of an incorrect address or because the recipient was unavailable, extra charges may apply to ship it again.

My parcel arrived damaged or something is missing

Report damaged or missing items within 48 hours of delivery. Share your order number, your unboxing video, and photos of the item and the packaging. We will offer a replacement or a refund. The assistant can register the claim for you.
