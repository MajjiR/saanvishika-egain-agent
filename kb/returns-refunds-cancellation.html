Returns, Refunds and Cancellation

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, until it is dispatched. After dispatch, an order cannot be cancelled. The assistant can cancel an order that has not shipped yet, or you can email saanvishika.co@gmail.com with your order details.

Can I return an item because I changed my mind?

No. We do not accept change-of-mind returns. Refunds and replacements are only for damaged, defective or incorrect items.

When can I get a refund or replacement?

You can get a replacement or a refund when the item you received is damaged, defective (for example a stone fell out or a clasp is broken) or incorrect (wrong design or colour).

  • Damaged in transit or missing items: report within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Defective or incorrect items: report within 7 days of delivery.
  • An unboxing video is required. Record a video while opening the parcel.

What is not covered?

  • Damage from misuse or improper care, for example contact with water, perfume or chemicals.
  • Custom-made or personalised pieces, unless they are defective.
  • Claims reported after the time limits above.

How long does a refund take?

After the returned item is received and inspected, we confirm approval. Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.

How do I return an item?

Register the claim with the assistant or email saanvishika.co@gmail.com with your order number, the reason, and your unboxing video. We send you the return address. Pack the item securely. It must be unused, with its original packaging and tags.
