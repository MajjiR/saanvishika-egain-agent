Collections and Shopping Guide

What do you sell?

Traditional and contemporary Indian imitation jewellery: necklaces and necklace sets, haram and long necklaces, chokers, jhumka earrings, chandbalis, danglers, stud earrings, ear chains, ear cuffs, kante, tikka, nose pins, septum rings, neck patti, choti, payal (anklets), pendants, beads mala and interchangeable jewellery.

What should I wear for a wedding?

For brides, see Bridal Jewellery: heavy necklace sets, haram, chokers, bridal ear chains, tikka and choti. For guests, chokers with studs, jhumkas and ear cuffs work well with sarees and lehengas.

Popular styles

  • Peacock designs: necklaces, pendants and earcuffs with peacock motifs.
  • Lakshmi kasu and antique coin necklaces, traditional for festivals and poojas.
  • Victorian finish sets with CZ stones.
  • Diamond-look CZ chokers, ear chains and studs for receptions.

What price ranges do you have?

Prices range from ₹299 to about ₹6,000. Shop by budget: below ₹499, below ₹999, below ₹1,999, below ₹2,999 and below ₹3,999. The assistant can suggest pieces within your budget.

What does Prebooking mean?

Prebooking items are new designs you can reserve before they arrive in stock. They ship once stock arrives.

Do the colours look exactly like the photos?

We try to show every piece accurately, but lighting, photography and screens can cause slight colour differences.
