Materials and Jewellery Care

What is Saanvishika jewellery made of?

Our pieces use high-quality imitation metals that are nickel-free, with premium gold or silver plating, and crystals, American Diamond (AD or CZ) stones, kundan, polki-look stones, gemstone-look stones and beads.

What finishes do you offer?

Finishes include antique gold, Victorian (dark gold), two-tone, gold-tone, platinum-look and panchloha-look. Each product page lists its finish and stones.

Is it real gold or real diamonds?

No. Saanvishika makes imitation (artificial) jewellery. "Diamond look", "AD" and "CZ" mean American Diamond or cubic zirconia stones, not real diamonds.

How do I care for my jewellery?

  • Put jewellery on last, after perfume, hairspray and make-up.
  • Keep it away from water, sweat and chemicals. Remove it before bathing, swimming or exercising.
  • After wearing, wipe it gently with a soft dry cloth.
  • Store each piece separately in its box or a zip pouch, away from air and moisture, to slow tarnishing.
  • Do not use jewellery cleaners or polish on plated pieces.

I have sensitive skin. Can I wear it?

Our metals are nickel-free, which suits most people. Skin reactions can still happen. If you get redness, itching or a rash, remove the jewellery and contact us. Our assistant cannot give medical advice and will pass the case to our team.

Do earcuffs need an extra piercing?

No. Our earcuffs hook over the ear while the stud sits in your regular piercing.
