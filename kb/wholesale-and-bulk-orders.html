Wholesale and Bulk Orders

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Resellers, boutiques, and anyone buying in bulk for events or gifting can get wholesale prices directly from our AI assistant, any time of day.

What is the minimum order?

Wholesale prices start at 10 pieces of a design.

What are the wholesale discounts?

  • 10 to 24 pieces: 10% off the website price
  • 25 to 49 pieces: 15% off
  • 50 to 99 pieces: 20% off
  • 100 pieces or more: 25% off

Website prices include GST. The assistant gives you an instant quote with a quote number, valid for 7 days.

Can I get a bigger discount?

The assistant can only offer the discount for your quantity. Requests above 25%, and orders above 500 pieces, go to the owner, who replies within one working day.

How do I confirm a wholesale order?

Send your quote number to us on WhatsApp at +91 81438 88287 or email saanvishika.co@gmail.com. We confirm stock, share payment details and give the dispatch date.

Do you offer custom designs or credit terms?

Custom designs, private labels and credit or payment terms are decided by our team personally. Ask the assistant to connect you.
